## Lichess chess openings dataset

DebutFlow includes a curated and reformatted subset of opening names and move sequences from:

- Project: `lichess-org/chess-openings`
- Repository: https://github.com/lichess-org/chess-openings
- Data license: CC0 1.0 / Public Domain Dedication

The upstream project describes the dataset as a collection of facts in the public domain and releases any copyrightable interest under CC0.

The DebutFlow application code, interface, training logic, Russian grouping names, comments generated by the application, and the particular application structure are separate from the upstream dataset.
